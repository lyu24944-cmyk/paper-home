# Product context

The current visual authority is the user's approved realistic cottage and courtyard reconstruction brief, replacing the earlier illustrated architecture direction.

- A Chinese personal home on the web, experienced by entering a full cottage, walking its corridor, reading, and viewing sample art.
- One 11 × 15 m single-storey house with a complete roof, exterior, porch and courtyard. Corridor width 2.2 m; room door depths 3.2, 7 and 10.8 m, left–right–left. Walking eye height approximately 1.65 m above the current ground.
- Natural daylight, warm brick, light plaster, dark gray roof, wood, physical glass, solid paving and three-dimensional plants. Existing artwork keeps its illustration style.
- Preserve scroll walking, maps, room return positions, reading, artwork viewing, local rename, local sound and reduced motion.
- Desktop first, with tablet, phone and narrow desktop window regression. Resolution and geometry budgets are explicit and measured.
- Content remains demonstrative. Keep Place and content interfaces compatible.
- Only a local, operable preview is authorized for this milestone. No extra rooms, backend or public deployment.

Success is a coherent residential space and reversible navigation: a visitor must keep a recognizable architectural anchor throughout transitions, reach a deterministic final camera pose, and regain all navigation controls. Failed resource loads must leave the visitor in a recognizable scene with retry available.
