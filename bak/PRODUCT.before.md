# Product context

Confirmed by the user's brief and accepted implementation proposal:

- Build a personal home on the web, inspired by the supplied 34-second video.
- The experience is entering a house, exploring its corridor and opening room doors.
- Preserve the hand-drawn architectural feel, a colored garden exterior, pale interior and animated camera movement.
- Final content is undecided. All initial articles and artwork are explicitly demonstrative and replaceable.
- The approved first milestone is a working local preview. Deployment and a personal domain follow review of the visual effect.
- Chinese is the primary interface language.

Implementation choices for this milestone: a study, a gallery and an empty room; controlled camera stops; a room map for direct and keyboard navigation; optional ambient sound; a locally saved house name. These are prototype choices, not permanent content commitments.
